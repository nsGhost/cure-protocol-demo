# Cure Protocol — playable demo

An isometric action game where you **cure** zombies instead of killing them.

**▶ Play in your browser: https://nsghost.github.io/cure-protocol-demo/**

Cure them all. Bring them home.

## The idea

Killing is fast, easy, and costs you. Curing is slow, dangerous, and worth points — and it turns a zombie into a fragile person who then has to be protected and walked to a safe zone. Every level gives you a **kill budget**; blow it and the level fails.

Five levels introduce one idea at a time: curing basics, protecting survivors, crowd pressure, fast Runners, and a final forced-triage stand. Watch for the **Infector** (re-infects the people you just saved) and the **Spitter** (punishes you for backing away while shooting).

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD | Move |
| Mouse | Aim (independent of movement) |
| LMB | Fire active tool |
| Q / 1 / 2 | Swap cure darts ↔ pistol |
| E / RMB | Cure pulse (AOE) |
| C | Shove (non-lethal) |
| Space | Dodge roll |
| Shift | Sprint |
| F | Send survivors to the safe zone |
| H | Toggle controls · Esc | Menu |

A gamepad works too — twin-stick aim, triggers to fire and pulse.

## Notes

- Click the page once before expecting sound; browsers block audio until you interact.
- Progress is saved in your browser, so it's separate from any desktop copy.
